Why This Extension

Built for people who keep many tabs open and want a cleaner, faster browser experience.

⏰ Smart Stale Detection

Set your own stale definition by hours or days and automatically flag tabs that have gone cold.

🧹 Fast Cleanup

Close stale tabs individually or in bulk from the extension popup and context actions, with undo support.

📊 Local-Only Analytics

Track closed stale tabs over the last 30 days without any cloud dependency.

Key Features

Everything you need to keep your browser tidy.

🔔 Smart Notifications

Get notified when stale tabs exceed your threshold with customizable cadence and snooze support.

🎯 Flexible Configuration

Customize stale thresholds, exclusions for pinned/muted tabs, and notification preferences.

⏮️ Undo Support

Accidentally closed the wrong tab? Undo your last action within a 10-second window.

🌐 Your Data Stays Yours

100% local processing with no cloud uploads, no tracking, and no external dependencies.
